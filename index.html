About the app

iicon news publisher is a private desktop tool used only by the iicon_news creator. It runs on the creator's own computer and is not offered to other users.

  • The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) to connect the iicon_news account.
  • The tool uploads finished videos made by iicon_news to that account through the Content Posting API, with the caption, privacy level and interaction settings the creator chooses before each post.
  • The tool reads the account's profile, follower count and the list of its own videos with view, like and comment counts (Display API) so the creator can see how each video performs.

The tool never posts to other people's accounts, never posts without the creator's action, and does not collect data about viewers.
