iicon news publisher · last updated 26 October 2026

1. About the app

iicon news publisher is a desktop app operated by iicon_news. It connects to a TikTok account through the official TikTok for Developers APIs, shows statistics about that account's videos and uploads finished videos to the account's TikTok drafts. The app is used for the iicon_news account and is not sold or offered as a public service.

2. Who may use it

By connecting a TikTok account you confirm that you own it or are authorized to manage it, and that you are at least 18 years old.

3. What the app does

  • Shows the connected account's profile and statistics, and the list of its public videos with their counts.
  • Uploads a video you choose to your TikTok drafts. You finish, edit and post the video yourself in the TikTok app.
  • It does not post, edit or delete videos on your profile and does not act on other accounts.

4. Your content

You keep all rights to your videos and text. You are responsible for making sure your content follows the TikTok Community Guidelines and TikTok Terms of Service, and that you have the rights to all footage, music and other material in it.

5. Disconnecting

You can disconnect at any time with “Disconnect” in the app or in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions. After that the app can no longer read data or upload videos.

6. No warranty

The app is provided as is. TikTok may change or limit its APIs at any time, and uploads may fail or be delayed. iicon_news is not liable for lost content, missed publication times or actions taken by TikTok on an account.
