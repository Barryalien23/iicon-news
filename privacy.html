Privacy Policy

iicon news publisher · last updated 26 October 2026

Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile: open ID, display name, avatar, profile link, bio and verification status (user.info.basic, user.info.profile).
  • Account statistics: follower, following, like and video counts (user.info.stats).
  • The list of the account's own public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts (video.list).
  • Access and refresh tokens that let the app upload videos you choose to your TikTok drafts (video.upload).

How it is used

Only to show you statistics about your own account and to upload the videos you choose to your drafts. The data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.

Where it is stored

Tokens and statistics are stored only on the computer where the app runs, in files readable only by that user account. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's own API.

How long it is kept

Tokens are kept until you disconnect the app or they expire. Statistics are shown live and are not kept as a separate database; a local log of uploads (time, file name and TikTok's upload status) is kept for the creator and can be deleted at any time.

Your choices

You can disconnect at any time with “Disconnect” in the app or in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions. After that the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the computer.

Children

The app is not meant for anyone under 18.
