Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile: open ID, display name, avatar, profile link, bio and verification status (user.info.basic, user.info.profile).
  • Account statistics: follower, following, like and video counts (user.info.stats).
  • The list of your own public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts (video.list).
  • Access and refresh tokens that let the tool upload and publish videos you choose (video.upload, video.publish).

How it is used

Only to publish your videos and to show you statistics about your own account. The data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train AI models.

Where it is stored

Tokens and statistics are stored only on the creator's own computer, in files readable only by that user account. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's own API.

How long it is kept

Tokens are kept until you disconnect the app or they expire. Statistics are kept as local reports for the creator and can be deleted at any time.

Your choices

You can revoke access in the TikTok app at any time (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions). After that, stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the computer.

Children

The tool is not meant for anyone under 18.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a direct message to @iicon_news on TikTok.
