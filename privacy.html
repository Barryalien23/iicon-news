Privacy Policy

iicon news publisher · last updated 3 November 2026

iicon_news connects two of its own accounts to official platform APIs: the TikTok account, through the iicon news publisher desktop app, and the Pinterest business account, through the team's private web dashboard. This policy explains what data each connection receives and how it is handled.

TikTok

Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile: open ID, display name, avatar, profile link, bio and verification status (user.info.basic, user.info.profile).
  • Account statistics: follower, following, like and video counts (user.info.stats).
  • The list of the account's own public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts (video.list).
  • Access and refresh tokens that let the app upload videos you choose to your TikTok drafts (video.upload).

How it is used

Only to show you statistics about your own account and to upload the videos you choose to your drafts. The data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.

Where it is stored

Tokens and statistics are stored only on the computer where the app runs, in files readable only by that user account. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's own API.

How long it is kept

Tokens are kept until you disconnect the app or they expire. Statistics are shown live and are not kept as a separate database; a local log of uploads (time, file name and TikTok's upload status) is kept for the creator and can be deleted at any time.

Your choices

You can disconnect at any time with “Disconnect” in the app or in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions. After that the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the computer.

Pinterest

Data we receive from Pinterest

  • Basic account information: username, display name, profile image and account type (user_accounts:read).
  • The account's own boards (boards:read, boards:write).
  • The account's own Pins and their analytics: impressions, saves, outbound clicks and video views (pins:read).
  • Access and refresh tokens that let the dashboard create Pins on the account's own boards (pins:write).

The dashboard does not read other users' Pins, boards or profiles and does not collect data about people who view or save our Pins.

How it is used

Only to publish iicon_news videos as Pins at the time the team schedules them and to show the team how those Pins perform. The data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.

Where it is stored

Tokens and Pin statistics are stored on a private server operated by iicon_news (hosted in the EU). The dashboard is protected by a password and is available only to the iicon_news team. Data is sent only to Pinterest's own API.

How long it is kept

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected or they expire. Pin statistics are kept as a history for comparing videos and are deleted within 30 days after the account is disconnected.

Your choices

The connection can be removed at any time with “Disconnect” in the dashboard or in Pinterest's account settings (connected apps). After that the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the server.

Children

The app is not meant for anyone under 18.
